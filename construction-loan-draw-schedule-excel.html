How to Create a Construction Draw Schedule in Excel (Formula Guide)

Whether you are a custom home builder managing cash flow, a real estate investor renovating a commercial asset, or a lender underwriting progress inspections, here is the complete formula blueprint to build a construction loan draw schedule in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

⚡ Quick Answer: How to Create a Construction Draw Schedule in Excel

To build a construction loan draw schedule template in Excel in 5 simple steps:

  1. Set Up Project Assumptions: Enter Total Loan Commitment in cell B1, Annual Interest Rate in B2, and Retainage Rate (e.g., 10%) in B3.
  2. List Milestones (Schedule of Values): Create row headers for Permits & Foundation, Framing, Rough MEP, Drywall & Finishes, and Final Inspection with percentage allocations.
  3. Calculate Retainage and Net Payouts: In Column D, calculate retainage: =Gross_Draw * $B$3. In Column E, calculate Net Payment to Contractor: =Gross_Draw - Retainage.
  4. Compute Cumulative Drawn Balance: In Column F, sum all previous draws: =SUM($C$10:C10) to determine the current interest-bearing balance.
  5. Calculate Monthly Interest Accrual: In Column G, compute interest-only debt service: =Cumulative_Balance * ($B$2 / 12).

1 Schedule of Values (SOV) & Progress Inspections

Before any funds are released, lenders require a detailed Schedule of Values (SOV). Rather than paying based on time, draws are disbursed strictly against verified physical completion. A third-party inspector evaluates the site against the draw request, confirming that foundation concrete has cured, lumber has been framed to code, or rough-in plumbing has passed municipal pressure tests before approving the disbursement.

2 Why Lenders Enforce a 10% Retainage Holdback

Retainage protects both the project owner and the lender. By withholding 10% from every contractor invoice, the project maintains an escrow cushion. At the end of construction, the total retainage pool is only released once:

  • A formal Certificate of Occupancy (CO) is issued by the local building department.
  • Final Unconditional Lien Waivers are signed by the general contractor and all major trade subcontractors.
  • A final title date-down endorsement confirms zero mechanic's liens have attached to the deed.
3 Managing the Capitalized Interest Reserve (Dutch vs. Actual)

Because building projects generate zero revenue until completion, lenders commonly incorporate an Interest Reserve into the loan commitment. Each month, as interest accrues on cumulative drawn funds, the lender bills the interest reserve account automatically. Using our dynamic Excel schedule ensures you size your interest reserve accurately without over-borrowing.

The Schedule of Values (SOV) is the master budget established before breaking ground that allocates the total project cost across individual building trades and milestones. A Draw Request (often submitted on AIA G702 / G703 forms) is an invoice requesting payment against specific line items on the SOV based on the percentage of work completed to date.
