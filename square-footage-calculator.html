How is Square Footage Calculated? (Formulas & Methods)

Square footage is a measurement of two-dimensional area. In the United States and Canada, square footage is the standard metric used in real estate home appraisals, carpet and flooring estimation, painting projects, and construction blueprints.

Square Footage (ft²) = Length (ft) × Width (ft)

If measurements are in inches, multiply Length (in) × Width (in) and divide by 144: Sq Ft = (Length × Width) ÷ 144.

Formulas for Different Room Geometries

Shape Area Formula Example Calculation
Rectangle / Square Length × Width 12 ft × 15 ft = 180 sq ft
L-Shaped Room (L1 × W1) + (L2 × W2) (10 × 12) + (8 × 10) = 200 sq ft
Triangle (Base × Height) ÷ 2 (16 ft × 10 ft) ÷ 2 = 80 sq ft
Circle (Patio/Turf) π × Radius² (3.14159 × r²) 3.14159 × (7 ft)² = 153.94 sq ft

Unit Conversion Quick Reference Guide

  • Square Feet to Square Yards: Divide by 9 (e.g., 450 sq ft ÷ 9 = 50 sq yards of carpet).
  • Square Feet to Square Meters: Divide by 10.7639 (e.g., 1,000 sq ft ÷ 10.7639 = 92.9 m²).
  • Square Feet to Acres: Divide by 43,560 (e.g., 21,780 sq ft ÷ 43,560 = 0.5 acres).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What counts toward a home's gross living area (GLA) square footage? +
Under ANSI (American National Standards Institute) real estate appraisal guidelines, finished square footage must be heated, cooled, fully contiguous, and above-grade with ceiling heights of at least 7 feet. Unfinished basements, garages, detached sheds, and open air porches cannot be included in official MLS heated square footage.
How do you calculate square footage with fractional inches? +
Convert inches to decimal fractions of a foot by dividing inches by 12: 3 inches = 0.25 ft, 6 inches = 0.50 ft, 9 inches = 0.75 ft. For example, a room measuring 11 feet 3 inches by 14 feet 6 inches equals 11.25 ft × 14.5 ft = 163.125 square feet.
How do you calculate the square footage of walls for painting? +
Calculate the room perimeter (2 × [Length + Width]) and multiply by the ceiling height. Then subtract the area of doors (standard 21 sq ft per door) and windows (standard 15 sq ft per window). For a dedicated estimator, use our Paint Cost & Estimate Calculator.
