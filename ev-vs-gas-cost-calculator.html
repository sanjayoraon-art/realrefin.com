EV Charging vs. Gas Cost & Cost Per Mile Explained

When shopping for a vehicle, sticker price alone hides the true 5-year cost. Our gas vs EV cost calculator models both your upfront net purchase price (after EV tax credits or subsidies) and your ongoing operating costs—specifically EV cost per mile vs gas and annual maintenance.

EV Cost Per Mile vs. Gas Cost Per Mile Formula

EV Cost/Mile = Blended Electricity Rate ($/kWh) ÷ EV Efficiency (mi/kWh)
Gas Cost/Mile = Gas Price ($/gal) ÷ Vehicle MPG
  • Home Charging Advantage: Charging overnight at residential utility rates ($0.12–$0.18/kWh) typically delivers an EV cost per mile that is 55%–70% lower than gasoline.
  • Public Fast Charging Impact: DC fast chargers cost 2x–3x more than home electricity; use the Public Fast-Charge Share (%) input above to model road trips or apartment living.
  • Maintenance Savings: EVs eliminate oil changes, spark plugs, transmission fluid, and reduce brake pad wear through regenerative braking.
