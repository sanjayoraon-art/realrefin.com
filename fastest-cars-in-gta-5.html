Frequently Asked Questions: Fastest & Best GTA 5 Cars

What car is the fastest in GTA 5 Story Mode? +
In offline Story Mode (playing as Michael, Franklin, or Trevor), the Truffade Adder (Bugatti Veyron) and the Pegassi Zentorno are the fastest supercars available without online DLC vehicles, reaching roughly 125 mph to 126 mph when fully customized at Los Santos Customs.
Why is the BF Weevil Custom faster than the Ocelot Pariah? +
When converted at Benny's Original Motor Works, the BF Weevil Custom benefits from a rare vehicle physics flag that generates extreme downforce-to-suspension compression at maximum speed, allowing it to clock 137.50 mph—edging out the Pariah by 1.50 mph.
What is the best starter car to buy in GTA Online? +
The Karin Armored Kuruma ($698,250 after the Fleeca Job Heist) is universally considered the best investment for any new player. Its windows are virtually 100% immune to enemy NPC bullets, allowing you to complete contact missions, VIP work, and heist setups with zero chance of dying.
What is Hao's Special Works (HSW) and is it on PC? +
Hao's Special Works (HSW) is an exclusive tuning shop located inside the LS Car Meet on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It allows selected vehicles (like the Stinger TT and Vigero ZX) to exceed 160+ mph. As of now, HSW upgrades are exclusive to ninth-generation consoles and are not available on the PC version of GTA Online.
