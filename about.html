About RealRefin.com

Institutional Real Estate Analytics Built for Everyday Investors

Our Mission

At RealRefin.com, our mission is straightforward: to replace slow, fragile, and bloated spreadsheet templates with ultra-fast, institutional-grade financial modeling software accessible to every real estate investor in the world—100% free of charge.

For decades, Wall Street private equity firms and commercial syndicates have used sophisticated debt amortization models and sensitivity analysis engines to evaluate acquisitions. Individual investors, landlords, and flippers, by contrast, were left wrestling with corrupted Excel workbooks on their smartphones during property walkthroughs. RealRefin levels the playing field by putting institutional computational power directly in your web browser.

Our Core Pillars

⚡

Zero-Latency Speed

Real-time client-side calculation engines that recalculate debt service, yield spreads, and cash-out proceeds on every keystroke without requiring server round-trips.

🔒

Absolute Privacy

Your property deal numbers never touch our servers. All financial arithmetic executes in your local browser sandbox. We never store, harvest, or monetize your deal data.

🌐

Built specifically for cross-border real estate investors with native currency support ($ US, £ UK, A$ Australia) and regional underwriting benchmarks.

Our Mathematical and Editorial Standards

Accuracy is paramount in real estate underwriting. An error of 25 basis points in an interest rate or an inaccurate vacancy assumption can lead to costly mistakes.

  • Standard Banking Formulas: All mortgage amortization engines utilize standard annuity formulas compliant with Fannie Mae, the Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority), and the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA).
  • Peer-Reviewed Calculations: Our algorithms are rigorously verified by real estate financial analysts and tested against actual bank settlement statements.
  • Continuous Regulatory Updates: We continuously monitor legislative and lending changes—such as UK Section 24 mortgage interest relief restrictions, US non-QM DSCR lender criteria, and Australian serviceability buffer rules—to keep our calculators current.

Our Business Model & Transparency

RealRefin.com is committed to remaining free for all investors. To fund our cloud infrastructure, ongoing development, and algorithmic maintenance, we display unobtrusive, contextual advertisements and may partner with select vetted lending platforms.

We maintain a strict firewall between our computational tools and commercial partnerships: advertiser relationships never influence our mathematical formulas, rating badges, or editorial analysis.
