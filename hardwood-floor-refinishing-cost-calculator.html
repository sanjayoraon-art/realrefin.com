How Much Does it Cost to Refinish Hardwood Floors in 2026?

The national average cost to professionally refinish solid hardwood floors ranges between $3.50 and $6.00 per square foot for complete drum sanding, staining, and three protective coats of commercial polyurethane. For an average 1,000-square-foot home, homeowners typically invest between $3,500 and $6,000.

Full Sand vs. Screen and Recoat: Which Do You Need?

Feature Screen and Recoat (Buffing) Full Sanding & Refinishing
Cost / Sq Ft $1.50 – $2.50 $3.50 – $6.00
Cuts Down to Bare Wood? No (preserves wood thickness) Yes (takes off 1/32" of raw wood)
Fixes Deep Scratches & Dents? No (only fixes cloudy clear coat) Yes (erases gouges, burns & stains)

Water-Based vs. Oil-Based Polyurethane

Water-based polyurethane (e.g. Bona Traffic HD) has become the gold standard among premium flooring contractors. It dries in 2 to 3 hours, emits virtually zero odor or toxic volatile organic compounds (VOCs), and will never yellow with age, allowing white oak to keep its natural contemporary aesthetic.

Oil-modified polyurethane cures to a traditional, rich amber hue that enhances red oak and antique heart pine. However, it requires 24 hours of drying time per coat and strong ventilation during the 7-day cure window.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can engineered hardwood floors be sanded and refinished? +
It depends entirely on the thickness of the top solid wood wear layer. Engineered flooring with a 3mm to 6mm top veneer can safely be sanded and refinished 2 to 4 times. However, budget engineered floors with a paper-thin wear layer (less than 2mm) cannot withstand a drum sander and can only undergo a gentle screen-and-recoat.
How much does it cost to refinish hardwood stairs? +
Hardwood stair treads must be hand-sanded with palm sanders and custom scraped around balusters and risers. Professional contractors charge between $45 and $85 per stair tread and riser combination. A typical flight of 12 to 14 stairs costs $600 to $1,100 to refinish.
Is dustless sanding worth the extra cost? +
Yes. Modern dustless refinishing uses high-powered trailer-mounted or portable HEPA vacuums connected directly to the sanding drums and edge sanders. It captures over 99% of airborne sawdust, preventing wood particles from circulating into your HVAC air ducts, walls, and home electronics.
