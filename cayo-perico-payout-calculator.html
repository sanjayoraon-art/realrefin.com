Cayo Perico Heist Guide: Maximize Every Run

The Cayo Perico Heist, introduced in GTA Online's December 2020 update, changed everything by allowing solo play and delivering consistently high payouts in just 15–25 minutes per run.

Primary Loot Ranking (Best to Worst)

  • Panther Statue (Event Only): Up to ~$1,900,000 on Hard. Spawns only during limited-time Rockstar events.
  • Pink Diamond: ~$1,430,000 on Hard. Best regularly-rotating primary loot.
  • Bearer Bonds: ~$1,100,000 on Hard. Second-best standard rotation.
  • Sapphire Necklace: ~$1,000,000 on Hard.
  • Ruby Necklace / Tequila: ~$900,000 on Hard.
  • Artwork / Weed / Cash: Lowest value. Avoid if possible.

How to Maximize Your Cayo Perico Payout

  1. Always choose Hard difficulty — the 10% bonus adds up to ~$150,000 extra per run.
  2. Scout the Island thoroughly — mark all secondary loot locations (especially Gold crates inside the compound).
  3. Prioritise Gold as secondary loot — highest GTA$ per unit at $332,184 per crate on Hard.
  4. Use the Drainage Tunnel approach — fastest and most reliable solo entry.
  5. Go solo (1 player) unless you have a very coordinated crew.
  6. Play during Rockstar Event Weeks — Panther Statue spawns and 2× GTA$ events can double effective hourly income.

Pavel's Cut: How Much Does He Take?

Pavel charges a fixed flat fee of $50,000 from every Cayo Perico payout — unlike the original heists where Lester takes a percentage cut.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the max Cayo Perico payout in GTA Online?+
The maximum standard payout is approximately $2,044,000 (Hard, Pink Diamond primary, Gold secondary, solo). During Panther Statue event weeks, payouts can reach ~$2,350,000+.
Is Cayo Perico still the best heist in 2026?+
Yes. Despite Rockstar's cooldown nerfs, Cayo Perico remains the #1 most efficient money-making heist in GTA Online thanks to solo capability, ~20-minute completion, and high base payout.
How long is the Cayo Perico cooldown between runs?+
Following Rockstar's cooldown patch, there is a 3-hour (180-minute) cooldown between consecutive Cayo Perico finale attempts on the same character. You can switch to an alternate character slot while your primary cooldown resets.
How many players is best for Cayo Perico?+
Solo (1 player) is best for maximum take-home cash per run. With 2+ players, total gross increases slightly but must be split, reducing individual earnings. Only group play is recommended for helping friends with first-time bonuses.
