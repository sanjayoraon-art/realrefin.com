No, there is no official cheat code, controller combination, or cell phone dial number that gives you money in GTA 5 Story Mode or GTA Online. To get max cash ($2.14 Billion per character), follow the Lester Assassination stock market investment method detailed in our guide above.
Do GTA 5 cheat codes disable trophies and achievements? +
Yes. The moment you activate any cheat code, all PlayStation Trophies and Xbox Achievements are locked for that gaming session. If you want to keep earning trophies, always create a manual save before using cheats, or reload your previous save file when finished.
Can I use cheat codes in GTA Online? +
No. Cheat codes only function in single-player Story Mode (Michael, Franklin, and Trevor). Entering cheat codes in GTA Online does not work, and third-party modding or money injection software is strictly prohibited by Rockstar Games and will lead to an account ban.
How long does the Invincibility (God Mode) cheat last? +
The Invincibility cheat code lasts for exactly 5 minutes. You will see a countdown timer in the bottom right corner of the screen. Once the timer expires, you must re-enter the code to regain invulnerability.
