4. Real Estate Investment Risks

Real estate acquisitions, fix-and-flip rehabs, and property refinancing carry substantial financial, market, and operational risks:

  • Appraisal Risk: After Repair Values (ARVs) are subjective estimates. Actual professional bank appraisals may be significantly lower, restricting the amount of cash that can be extracted via refinance.
  • Rehabilitation Overruns: Hidden structural defects, contractor disputes, material inflation, and permitting delays routinely cause construction budgets to exceed initial estimates.
  • Interest Rate & Lending Volatility: Mortgage interest rates, loan-to-value (LTV) limits, and debt coverage requirements fluctuate based on central bank monetary policy and lender underwriting standards.
  • Tenant Default & Vacancy: Rental cash flow depends on timely tenant payments and minimal vacancy intervals. Extended evictions, legal costs, and uncollected rent can result in negative cash flow.

5. Mandatory Professional Consultation

Before signing real estate purchase contracts, executing loan agreements, or allocating investment capital, you must consult qualified independent professionals licensed in your jurisdiction:

  • A certified real estate appraiser or chartered surveyor (RICS) for property valuations.
  • A licensed mortgage loan officer or independent debt broker for lending commitments.
  • A certified public accountant (CPA) or chartered tax advisor for tax optimization.
  • A qualified real estate attorney or conveyancing solicitor for legal contracts and title due diligence.
