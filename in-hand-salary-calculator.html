How Does the In Hand Salary Calculator India Work?

The in hand salary calculator india bridges the gap between what companies promise as Cost to Company (CTC) and what actually lands in your bank account on payday. Many job seekers are surprised when an offer letter of ₹12 Lakhs CTC results in a monthly salary of ₹82,000–₹85,000 rather than ₹1,00,000.

Using our ctc to in hand salary calculator, you can calculate the exact deductions for Employee Provident Fund (EPF), Gratuity holdbacks, Professional Tax, and Income Tax TDS under both the New Tax Regime (Budget 2024–2026 revisions) and the Old Tax Regime.

In Hand Salary Calculation Formula in India

Understanding how your salary is computed involves a three-stage mathematical equation:

$$\text{In Hand (Take-Home) Salary} = \text{Gross Monthly Salary} - (\text{Employee PF} + \text{Professional Tax} + \text{Monthly TDS})$$

Where Gross Salary is determined by subtracting non-cash employer components from CTC:

$$\text{Gross Salary} = \text{CTC} - (\text{Employer PF Contribution} + \text{Gratuity} + \text{Employer Medical/Insurance})$$

New Tax Regime vs Old Tax Regime Slabs (FY 2025–26)

Under the revised New Tax Regime introduced by the Ministry of Finance, the standard deduction for salaried taxpayers is ₹75,000, with tax rebates ensuring zero income tax on taxable earnings up to ₹7,00,000 (effective zero tax up to ₹7,75,000 for salaried employees).

Detailed CTC Component Breakdown

  • Basic Salary: The core foundation of your salary structure, typically 40% to 50% of CTC. Both Employee PF (12%) and Gratuity (4.81%) are legally pegged to this amount.
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA): Designed to cover accommodation costs. Under the Old Tax Regime, HRA is partially or fully exempt under Section 10(13A). Under the New Tax Regime, no HRA exemption is permitted.
  • Employee Provident Fund (EPF): 12% of basic salary is deducted directly from your earnings and transferred into your EPFO account, building retirement savings with government-backed compound interest.
  • Gratuity: Regulated by the Payment of Gratuity Act, employers allocate 4.81% of your basic pay into a gratuity reserve, payable after 5 years of continuous service.
  • Professional Tax (PT): A state government levy (maximum ₹2,500/year, usually ₹200/month) deducted from monthly gross pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is in hand salary calculated from CTC in India? +
In hand salary is calculated by deducting statutory deductions (Employee PF, Professional Tax, TDS/Income Tax) and employer components (Employer PF, Gratuity, Insurance) from your Gross Cost to Company (CTC). The basic formula is: In Hand Salary = Gross Monthly Salary - (Employee PF + Professional Tax + Monthly TDS).
Why is in hand salary much lower than annual CTC? +
Annual CTC includes non-cash items such as Employer PF contributions (12%), Gratuity reserves (4.81%), and insurance coverages. In addition, Employee PF, Professional Tax, and Income Tax TDS are deducted from your gross pay, which typically makes monthly take-home pay 15% to 30% lower than CTC ÷ 12.
Which tax regime gives more in hand salary in FY 2025-26? +
For individuals earning up to ₹15 Lakhs without massive tax exemptions, the New Tax Regime offers higher take-home salary due to lower slab rates, an enhanced ₹75,000 standard deduction, and zero tax on income up to ₹7.75 Lakhs. If you have over ₹3.75 Lakhs in combined 80C, 80D, HRA, and home loan interest deductions, the Old Regime may be better.
How much in hand salary will I get for 12 LPA CTC? +
For a 12 LPA CTC in India under the New Tax Regime, your gross monthly pay is ~₹93,000. After deducting Employee PF (~₹4,800), Professional Tax (₹200), and monthly TDS (~₹5,000), your monthly take-home salary will be approximately ₹82,000 to ₹85,500.
What percentage of in hand salary should go toward a Home Loan EMI? +
Underwriters recommend keeping total monthly debt obligations below 35% to 40% of net monthly in-hand salary. On a take-home salary of ₹1,00,000/month, your ideal home loan EMI should not exceed ₹35,000 to ₹40,000.
