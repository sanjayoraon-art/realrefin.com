Frequently Asked Questions

According to CDC and American Academy of Sleep Medicine guidelines, most adults (18–64) need 7 to 9 hours per night (5 to 6 complete 90-minute sleep cycles), teens need 8–10 hours, and school-age children need 9–12 hours.
Each cycle moves from light sleep to deep slow-wave sleep and REM dreaming. Waking up at the end of a 90-minute cycle minimizes sleep inertia (morning grogginess) compared to waking in the middle of deep sleep.
The average healthy adult takes 10 to 20 minutes to transition from lying in bed to entering Stage 1 sleep (sleep latency). You can customize this buffer from 5 to 35 minutes above.
