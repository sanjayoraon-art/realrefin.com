How to Calculate Age Between Two Dates (Step-by-Step Formula)

Manual chronological subtraction can be tricky because calendar months vary between 28, 29, 30, and 31 days. When calculating age between two dates:

  • Step 1 (Days): Subtract the birth day from the target day. If negative, borrow the exact number of days in the preceding calendar month.
  • Step 2 (Months): Subtract the birth month from the target month (borrowing 12 months and reducing years by 1 if negative).
  • Step 3 (Years & Leap Days): Subtract the birth year from the target year. Total days, weeks, and hours automatically include every February 29 leap day between the two dates.
