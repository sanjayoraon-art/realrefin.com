What is a Mid Term Rental (MTR) and Why Is It Booming?

A mid term rental (MTR) is a fully furnished residential property leased for flexible stays ranging between 30 days and 6 months (averaging 90 days). In the post-2024 real estate landscape, the mid-term rental strategy has emerged as the premier sweet spot between traditional long-term rentals (LTR) and highly regulated short-term rentals (STR / Airbnb).

Using our institutional mid term rental calculator, real estate investors and master lease operators can underwrite travel nurse housing, corporate relocation contracts, insurance displacement assignments, all-inclusive utility expenses, and upfront furniture ROI with surgical precision.

💡 The MTR Regulatory & Tax Advantage

Because mid-term rental leases strictly exceed 30 consecutive days, they are legally classified as residential tenancies in nearly all US municipal codes. This exempts your property from municipal short-term rental bans, strict Airbnb lottery permits, and expensive 10%–15% local hotel / transient occupancy taxes (TOT).

How to Calculate Mid Term Rental Cash Flow

Calculating accurate cash flow on an MTR requires accounting for unique operating dynamics that traditional rental calculators ignore:

1. Effective Gross Income & Realistic Occupancy

Travel nurses and corporate executives typically sign 13-week contracts (approximately 90 days). Landlords experience a 7 to 14 day turnover vacancy window between tenants for cleaning, staging maintenance, and lease execution. A conservative, institutional-grade underwriting model applies an 85% to 90% occupancy rate (equivalent to 10 to 11 occupied months per year) rather than assuming 100% full-year occupancy.

2. Landlord All-Inclusive Utilities

In mid term rentals, the landlord pays all utilities to provide a turnkey move-in experience. Your monthly budget must include electric, natural gas, water/sewer/trash, streaming subscriptions, and high-speed gigabit Wi-Fi (a mandatory requirement for remote workers). To mitigate utility abuse, experienced operators incorporate an excess utility clause capping monthly electrical and gas usage at $150 to $250, billing the guest for any overages.

3. Upfront Furnishing Amortization & Payback Period

The upfront cost to furnish a property ($6,000 to $15,000) represents the primary barrier to entry. Our calculator computes your exact Furniture Payback Period: the number of months required for your extra MTR cash flow (over a baseline unfurnished long-term lease) to fully repay your out-of-pocket setup capital.

Mid Term Rental vs. Long Term Rental vs. Short Term Rental

The table below summarizes the key financial and operational trade-offs across all three rental strategies:

Mid Term Rental (MTR) 30 – 120 Days 1.4x – 2.0x of LTR Low (3 to 4 tenant turnovers per year; professional corporate & healthcare guests). Favorable. Exempt from short-term rental bans & hotel taxes in 95% of US cities.

Top 4 High-Demand Mid Term Rental Tenant Avatars

To maximize occupancy on Furnished Finder and Airbnb 30+, align your property's amenities with one of these primary MTR tenant profiles:

  • Traveling Healthcare Professionals: Travel nurses, locum tenens physicians, and physical therapists seeking 13-week furnished leases within a 15-minute commute of major hospital centers. Priority amenities: blackout bedroom curtains, in-unit washer/dryer, and ultra-quiet sleeping environments.
  • Insurance Displacement (ALE Housing): Families displaced by house fires, water pipe bursts, or storm damage whose temporary living expenses (ALE) are funded directly by insurance carriers (State Farm, Allstate, Travelers). These represent the highest-paying MTR contracts ($4,000 to $8,000+/month), often extending for 4 to 9 months.
  • Corporate Relocations & Remote Executives: Digital nomads, corporate transferees, and consultants on multi-month project assignments. Priority amenities: dedicated ergonomic workspace, dual-monitor setup, standing desk, and verified 300+ Mbps Wi-Fi.
  • Home Renovators & In-Between Buyers: Local homeowners undergoing major kitchen or whole-home remodeling projects, or home buyers waiting for custom home construction or escrow closings.
