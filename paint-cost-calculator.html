How to Calculate Paint Cost and Gallons: Formulas & Coverage Rules

Accurately determining how many gallons of paint and primer you need prevents running out midway through a coat or over-purchasing custom-tinted cans that cannot be returned. Professional paint estimators rely on these exact mathematical formulas:

Paint Square Footage & Gallon Calculation Formula

1. Wall Perimeter = 2 × (Room Length + Room Width)
2. Gross Wall Area = Perimeter × Ceiling Height
3. Deductions = (Doors × 21 sq ft) + (Windows × 15 sq ft)
4. Net Wall Area = Gross Wall Area - Deductions
5. Ceiling Area = Room Length × Room Width (if painting ceiling)
6. Total Paintable Area = Net Wall Area + Ceiling Area + Trim Area
7. Gallons of Paint = ⌈ (Total Area × Number of Coats) ÷ Coverage per Gallon (350 sq ft) ⌉

Example Calculation: Standard 12' × 12' Bedroom with 8' Ceilings

  • Wall Perimeter: 2 × (12 + 12) = 48 linear feet.
  • Gross Wall Area: 48 ft × 8 ft = 384 square feet.
  • Deductions: 1 standard entry door (21 sq ft) + 1 standard window (15 sq ft) = 36 sq ft.
  • Net Wall Area: 384 - 36 = 348 net sq ft.
  • Wall Gallons (2 Coats): (348 sq ft × 2) ÷ 350 = 1.98 → 2 Gallons.
  • Ceiling Area: 12 ft × 12 ft = 144 sq ft.
  • Ceiling Gallons (1-2 Coats): (144 sq ft × 1) ÷ 350 = 0.41 → 1 Gallon flat ceiling paint.
  • Total Paint Order: 2 gallons wall paint + 1 gallon ceiling paint.

Frequently Asked Questions About Painting Costs & Estimating

How much does it cost to paint a room in 2026? +
In 2026, the national average cost to professionally paint a standard 12'×12' bedroom ranges between $450 and $950 for walls only ($1.50 to $2.50 per square foot of wall area). If you include the ceiling, baseboard trim, door frames, and 2 full coats of premium paint, the turnkey cost ranges from $750 to $1,350. Doing it yourself typically costs between $120 and $220 for 2 gallons of quality paint, rollers, painter's tape, and drop cloths.
How many square feet does 1 gallon of paint cover? +
One standard gallon of paint covers approximately 350 to 400 square feet for a single coat on smooth, primed drywall. On rough, textured, porous, or unprimed surfaces, coverage drops to 250 to 300 square feet per gallon. Because durable coverage and drastic color transitions require 2 coats, plan on 1 gallon for every 175 to 200 square feet of actual wall space.
How much does a painter charge per square foot? +
Professional interior painters charge between $1.25 and $3.50 per square foot of paintable surface area (or $35 to $65 per hour per painter). Painting walls only averages $1.25 to $2.00/sq ft; adding ceilings costs an additional $0.80 to $1.20/sq ft; and detailed trim, crown molding, and doors cost $1.50 to $3.00 per linear foot or $40 to $70 per door.
Are 2 coats of paint always necessary? +
In 90% of residential projects, yes. Two coats ensure rich, uniform color depth, hide roller lap marks, and provide a thicker, scrub-resistant protective barrier. A single coat is only sufficient when refreshing a wall with the exact same color and brand in good condition without patching.
What paint sheen should I use for each room? +
Choose your sheen based on lighting and moisture: (1) Flat / Matte: Zero reflection, hides drywall blemishes; ideal for ceilings and low-traffic master bedrooms. (2) Eggshell / Satin: Soft pearl sheen, easily wipeable; industry standard for living rooms, hallways, and kids' bedrooms. (3) Semi-Gloss: Moisture-resistant and scrubbable; required for kitchens, bathrooms, baseboards, doors, and window trim.
How much does it cost to paint a 2,000 square foot house? +
To paint the entire interior of an average 2,000 square foot home (approx. 5,000 to 6,000 square feet of wall, ceiling, and trim surface), professional painters charge between $4,500 and $9,500. For the exterior of a 2,000 sq ft home (siding, soffits, fascia, trim), turnkey professional painting costs $3,500 to $7,200 depending on stories, pressure washing prep, and wood repairs.
