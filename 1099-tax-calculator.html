How Our 2026 1099 Tax Calculator Works

Unlike W-2 employees whose employers split payroll taxes, 1099-NEC independent contractors, freelancers, and gig workers (Uber, DoorDash, consultants) are responsible for both the employer and employee portions of Social Security and Medicare—known as the 15.3% self-employment tax. Our 1099 tax calculator computes your exact net Schedule C profit, applies the IRS 92.35% SE multiplier, deducts half of your SE tax, applies the 20% Section 199A QBI deduction, and divides your annual liability into quarterly Form 1040-ES payments.

Self-Employment Tax Formula (Schedule SE)

Net Profit = Gross 1099 Income − Business Deductions
SE Taxable Base = Net Profit × 0.9235
Self-Employment Tax = (12.4% Social Security up to Wage Base) + (2.9% Medicare)

Frequently Asked Questions

Most 1099 contractors set aside 25% to 30% of their net profit to cover the 15.3% self-employment tax plus federal and state income taxes. Use the SET ASIDE percentage badge above for your exact income level.
The IRS applies 15.3% (12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare) to 92.35% of your net Schedule C business profit, and allows you to deduct 50% of that SE tax from your adjusted gross income.
Standard Schedule C deductions include business mileage, home office expenses, laptops/software, client travel, health insurance premiums, and Solo 401(k) or SEP IRA retirement contributions.
Under Section 199A, eligible freelancers and sole proprietors can deduct up to 20% of qualified business income from federal taxable income, reducing their effective federal tax bill.
