2026 Tax Planning Strategies: How to Legally Lower Your Tax Bill

Proactive tax planning is the difference between overpaying the government in April and compounding those savings into long-term wealth. Whether you are a salaried professional, business owner, or investor, structuring your income around pre-tax accounts and allowable deductions lowers both your current marginal bracket and lifetime tax drag.

7 High-Impact Tax Planning Levers

  • Maximize Pre-Tax Retirement Accounts: Contributing to a 401(k) or Solo 401(k) in the US—or NPS 80CCD(1B) and EPF in India—reduces taxable income dollar-for-dollar at your highest marginal bracket.
  • Health Savings Account (HSA) Triple Tax Advantage: Contributions are tax-deductible, growth is tax-free, and qualified medical withdrawals are tax-free.
  • Tax-Loss Harvesting: Selling losing positions in a taxable brokerage account offsets capital gains and up to $3,000 of ordinary income per year.
  • Charitable Bunching & Donor-Advised Funds: Combine 2–3 years of charitable donations into one tax year to exceed the standard deduction threshold.
  • Section 80C, 80D & Regime Optimization (India): Compare the New Tax Regime vs. Old Tax Regime after factoring in ELSS, PPF, health insurance, and home loan interest.
